# CIRO (Crisis Intelligence & Response Orchestrator) Demo Video Script

**Duration**: ~5 Minutes
**Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges & Disaster Management Stakeholders (NDMA, PMD, NASA FIRMS)

---

## Part 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Opening shot of the CIRO Admin Panel Dashboard. Dark mode dashboard with live satellite overlay, active markers flashing, and live metrics showing affected population and economic losses.
- **Narrator**: 
  > "Disasters don't wait for us to solve them one by one. In the face of catastrophic cascading events like floods, wildfire outbreaks, and extreme heatwaves, traditional sequential emergency responses fall apart. Welcome to CIRO: the Crisis Intelligence & Response Orchestrator. CIRO is an agentic, multi-agent AI system designed to coordinate and manage multiple concurrent crises in real-time, built using Google's Antigravity AI runtime."
- **Action**: Mouse hovering over the side navigation, displaying the drawer menu. Toggling between the crises list, live map, citizen reports, and resource management dashboard.

---

## Part 2: Reporting & Citizen Verification (0:45 - 1:45)
- **Visual**: Switch view to the **Citizen Reports** tab. Show an unverified report of flash flooding near Nala Lai, Rawalpindi.
- **Narrator**:
  > "Every crisis begins with data. CIRO ingests telemetry feeds from satellites (NASA FIRMS), weather services (PMD), and crowd-sourced citizen reports. When a citizen submits a report with text and images, CIRO’s verification agent goes to work. By cross-referencing PMD precipitation sensors and conducting vision analysis on the uploaded images, the verifier computes a credibility score."
- **Action**: Click the **Verify** button. Show the pipeline executing in real-time, retrieving PMD rainfall data (e.g., 45mm/hr) and computing a 94% confidence score. Click **Promote to Crisis**.
- **Narrator**:
  > "Once validated, the report is instantly promoted to an active crisis, triggering the multi-agent planning pipeline."

---

## Part 3: Multi-Agent Orchestration & Decision Comparison (1:45 - 3:00)
- **Visual**: Switch to the **Comparison Screen**. Select the newly created crisis.
- **Action**: Point out the side-by-side comparison of **AI Agent Plan** vs. **Baseline (Rule-Based)**.
- **Narrator**:
  > "Unlike static, rule-based baselines that allocate resources using simple thresholds, CIRO deploys a coalition of specialized agents under a Multi-Crisis Coordinator. We can compare the decisions side-by-side: The rule-based system allocates generic ambulances based on severity. The AI Agent, however, fuses NASA hotspot data, PMD forecasts, and local population density to deploy specialized boats, dewatering pumps, and security details with a 92% confidence score, resolving resource conflicts across concurrent crises."

---

## Part 4: Dynamic Loss Aggregation & Routing (3:00 - 4:15)
- **Visual**: Click on the **Loss & Impact Simulation** screen. Show the dynamic losses:
  - Economic Loss (PKR millions)
  - Environmental Loss (hectares/forest index)
  - Traffic Delay Loss (hours)
  - Logistical Supply Chain Impact
- **Narrator**:
  > "CIRO doesn't just react; it simulates. The Loss Aggregator Projects the 24-hour cascading impact if no action is taken vs. with AI mitigation. Additionally, dispatchers get an interactive Routing Options Panel. Toggling from the 'Fastest Expressway' to the 'Flood Evacuation Detour' instantly updates satellite routing markers and recalculates ETAs, ensuring responders avoid flooded corridors."
- **Action**: Toggle routing strategies (Fastest, Flood Evac, Escorted) on the map and watch the ETA update from 8 mins to 14 mins dynamically.

---

## Part 5: Stakeholder Messaging & Mobile App (4:15 - 5:00)
- **Visual**: Show the **Messaging Hub** displaying custom safety messages tailored for different audiences (Local Citizens, Rescue Teams, Government Executives).
- **Narrator**:
  > "Communication is key to saving lives. CIRO automatically constructs customized advisories: Urdu alerts for citizens, tactical coordinates for responders, and policy updates for NDMA executives. These alerts sync instantly to our mobile companion app."
- **Visual**: Show screenshots/recording of the CIRO Mobile App: the sliding drawer sidebar, live environment dashboard tiles, and the interactive tactical checklist for field responders.
- **Narrator**:
  > "CIRO orchestrates intelligence so that humans can orchestrate response. With Google Antigravity, we turn chaotic data streams into lives saved."
